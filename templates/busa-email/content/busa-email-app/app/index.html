How It Works

/busa-email reads mail into Busabase. This AirApp proposes review decisions; after they are reviewed, ask /busa-email to execute approved decisions.

Workflow

  1. Ask /busa-email to generate a review batch.
  2. Review messages here and approve, request a draft, or leave a note.
  3. Ask /busa-email to execute approved decisions.
